# Blocksmith Name and Marks

The source licenses allow honest forks. They do not grant permission to imply that a fork, service, or modified release is the original Blocksmith project or is endorsed by its maintainers.

Forks should use a clearly different product name and remove Blocksmith logos and release-signing identifiers while preserving license notices and source attribution. Descriptive statements such as “based on Blocksmith” remain compatible with honest attribution.

This provisional policy should receive legal review before the first public release.
