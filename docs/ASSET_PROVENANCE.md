## Why these files are held

General project history supports Rich Henson's direction and AI-assisted creation, but it does not conclusively bind each binary file to an exact creator, date, clean-room prompt, reference list, and export event. A defensible manifest must not convert an inference into a fact.

## Replacement standard

Replacement assets must be newly generated or photographed specifically for Blocksmith for Hat Makers without competitor screenshots, copyrighted catalog art, recognizable third-party product photography, logos, or named living-artist imitation. Preserve for each asset:

- filename and purpose;
- creation date;
- creator/tool;
- complete prompt or photography notes;
- every input/reference asset and its permission basis;
- human selection and modifications;
- original output and final optimized output;
- dimensions, format, and SHA-256 hash; and
- license/release designation.

Until that process is complete, these six files are development references only and are not cleared public-release assets.
