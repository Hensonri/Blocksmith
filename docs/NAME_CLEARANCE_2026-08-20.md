## Conclusion

The exact phrase **“Blocksmith for Hat Makers”** did not surface as a prior product or service in the searches recorded below. The unqualified word **BLOCKSMITH**, however, is already used and federally registered by Blocksmith, Inc. in connection with a community focused on creating and sharing virtual-reality software and experiences. Public records also show earlier Blocksmith, Inc. filings for downloadable software and platform services used to create, upload, and share VR experiences; those particular filings appear dead or cancelled, but the underlying business and common-law use remain relevant.

Because this project is also 3D-design software, the overlap is more than nominal. Adding “for Hat Makers” narrows the audience and purpose, but it does not eliminate the identical dominant word. This project should not make a final availability claim or invest materially in the name until a U.S.-licensed trademark attorney reviews likelihood of confusion and common-law use.
