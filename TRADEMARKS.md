# Blocksmith for Hat Makers — Name and Marks Policy

This project currently uses the intended full name **Blocksmith for Hat Makers**. A preliminary search identified an existing BLOCKSMITH registration and adjacent historical/common-law use involving 3D creation software. The name is therefore **not represented as legally cleared** until a U.S.-licensed trademark attorney completes the review recorded in `docs/NAME_CLEARANCE_2026-08-20.md`.

No registration is presently claimed. Do not use the registered symbol `®`. Do not use this policy as evidence that the name is available or registered.

The project licenses permit honest forks and modification of covered source. They do not grant trademark rights or permission to imply that a fork, hosted service, installer, modified build, social account, or physical product is the original project or is endorsed by Rich Henson or the maintainers.

Pending final clearance, public references should use **Blocksmith for Hat Makers** in full. Forks should adopt a clearly different product name, remove Blocksmith-specific logos and release-signing identifiers, and retain the applicable copyright, source, and license notices. Accurate statements such as “based on Blocksmith for Hat Makers” are permitted when they do not imply endorsement.

Third-party names may be used only as necessary for accurate, non-misleading description or attribution. Do not include third-party logos, distinctive trade dress, catalog imagery, or branding in presets or releases without documented permission.

Questions about name use should be directed to the project maintainer. This policy does not grant rights in any third party's mark.
